# Pictures needed

Every page of the 79 storybooks, 202 in all, with the scene its picture should show. Until a new drawing is made, each page borrows one of the 60 drawings (shown in brackets).

**To add drawings:** draw them in the same style as the first three sheets (bold black outlines, white inside, no shading, a little ground or sky around the subject), 20 to a sheet in 4 rows of 5 boxes with the picture's name printed at the top of each box, like the animals sheet. Each new sheet goes in `coe/pictures/source/`, its names go in `tools/trace_pictures.py`, and the tracer cuts out, cleans and traces every drawing; then each page is pointed at its new picture.
